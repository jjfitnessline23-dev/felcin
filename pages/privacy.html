Last updated: May 12, 2026

Felcin ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Felcin social media platform, accessible at felcin.com and through our mobile apps. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and profile photo when you register.
  • Content you post: photos, videos, stories, reels, comments, and live streams you share on Felcin.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, and interactions (likes, follows, comments) within the app.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and browser type for technical support and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Felcin platform and its features.
  • To display your profile and content to other users according to your settings.
  • To send notifications about activity on your account (likes, comments, follows).
  • To improve the app, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • To ensure safety and enforce our community guidelines.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information in the following cases:

  • With other users: your public profile, posts, and content are visible to other Felcin users.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time, which removes your personal data from our systems.

5. User-Generated Content

Felcin allows users to post photos, videos, and text. You are responsible for the content you share. We reserve the right to remove content that violates our community guidelines. Our platform is rated 17+ due to user-generated content.

6. Children's Privacy

Felcin is not intended for users under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

  • Access and update your personal information via Profile Settings.
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time.
  • Contact us to request a copy of your data or to raise a privacy concern.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including Firebase App Check, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and Firebase Authentication to protect your account and data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes. Continued use of Felcin after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
