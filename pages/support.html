Support

We're here to help. Find answers below or contact us directly.

Contact Us

Have a question, problem, or feedback? Send us an email and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.

support@felcin.com

Report Objectionable Content

If you see a post that violates our Community Guidelines, tap the flag icon on the post to report it. You can also email us at support@felcin.com with a description of the content.

We review all reports and act on confirmed violations within 24 hours, including removing content and suspending accounts.

Block or Report a User

To block or report another user, go to their profile, tap the report button, and follow the prompts. Blocked users cannot see your content or interact with you, and their posts are removed from your feed immediately.

Account Issues

  • Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the login screen.
  • Can't log in? Email us at support@felcin.com.
  • Want to delete your account? Email us and we'll process the request within 24 hours.

Community Guidelines

Felcin does not tolerate objectionable content or abusive behavior. Review our full Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
